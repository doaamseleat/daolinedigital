Link Search Console to GA4: Find Organic Queries and Landing Pages

A practical guide to connect Search Console and GA4, read organic queries and landing pages, and turn impressions, clicks, engagement, and conversions into decisions.
By Doaa Seleat • Reviewed September 30, 2026
GA4 can show what visitors did on your website, but it cannot independently tell you the Google query that produced each visit. Search Console covers discovery before the click. Linking them creates a more useful decision view.
01

Use each product for its job

Search Console reports queries, impressions, clicks, countries, devices, pages, and average position in Google Search. GA4 reports sessions, engagement, events, and conversions on the site. Neither replaces the other.

02

Connect the correct properties

In GA4, open Admin, Product links, then Search Console Links. Select the verified Search Console property and the matching web stream. You generally need verified-owner access in Search Console and Editor access in Analytics.

Action: Write the reader decision before producing the asset.

03

Publish the two reports

Google Organic Search Queries explains discovery terms. Google Organic Search Traffic explains landing-page behavior. Add the collection from the GA4 Library if it is not visible in the reporting navigation.

04

Run a weekly decision review

Group queries by topic and page. High impressions with weak CTR may need a clearer title. Positions 8–20 may need deeper answers and internal links. Clicks with poor engagement may signal an intent mismatch.

05

Do not force the numbers to match

Search Console clicks and GA4 sessions use different definitions, processing, privacy rules, and time handling. Look for directional patterns and investigate material gaps instead of expecting identical totals.

Reminder: One signal is not enough; combine source, behavior, and outcome.

06

Connect SEO to outcomes

Define meaningful events such as qualified form submissions, bookings, or downloads. Record the change date whenever you update a page, then evaluate over a sufficient period rather than reacting to daily noise.

Frequently asked questions

Does linking improve rankings?

No. The connection improves reporting and does not create a ranking advantage.

Why are the reports empty?

The link may still be processing, the collection may not be published, or the selected properties may not match.
